Box scores14

Game and season box scores.

  • CorrectedKaggle box-score dataset (copy of NBA records)

    Stored final scores are wrong in 33 games (20 from 1996-2018, 13 in 1989), including two stored ties.

    Would distort: Standings (2004-05 Portland one win short, Toronto one over) and game margins.

  • Kept out of the analysisKaggle box-score dataset (copy of NBA records)

    1982-83 box scores lack offensive and defensive rebound splits on 5,309 rows, and some steals.

    Would distort: Rebound and steal rates in any study that starts in 1982-83.

  • Kept out of the analysisKaggle box-score dataset (copy of NBA records)

    From 1976 to 1985, some scorers show 0 minutes in nearly every game (one 1984-85 player: 0 minutes in 80 games).

    Would distort: Per-minute rates for about 250 player-seasons in 1982-85.

  • CorrectedNBA.com

    Box-score minutes are written as "22:60" instead of "23:00" on about 60 rows in 2024-26.

    Would distort: Minutes totals, by 0.4 minutes per affected row, when parsed as written.

  • Kept out of the analysisKaggle box-score dataset (copy of NBA records)

    Stats the league did not record are stored as 0: blocks and steals before 1973-74, turnovers before 1977-78, plus-minus before 1996-97, game minutes before 1982-83. About 60,000 early game rows show impossible shooting lines, such as makes above attempts.

    Would distort: Any shooting or per-minute study before 1982-83.

  • Kept out of the analysisKaggle box-score dataset (copy of NBA records)

    1946-54 coverage is thin (1949-50: 122 of 561 games), and George Mikan carries 7 duplicate rows copied from Ed Mikan.

    Would distort: Career totals that reach back to 1946-54.

  • CorrectedKaggle box-score dataset (copy of NBA records)

    Label slips: Goga Bitadze stored as "ga" on 470 rows, blank playoff labels in 1982-95, and two spellings of preseason.

    Would distort: Name lookups and game-type filters.

  • CorrectedKaggle box-score dataset (copy of NBA records)

    1984-85 team rebound totals include team rebounds: 6.77 more per team-game than the player sums.

    Would distort: Team rebound rates in 1984-85.

  • CorrectedKaggle box-score dataset (copy of NBA records)

    Final scores are rotated or swapped in 6 games (games 5-7 of a 1981 East semifinal and 3 others), and one 1974-75 record reads 41-41 instead of 40-42.

    Would distort: Margins and records in those seasons.

  • CorrectedNBA.com

    Team turnovers in the 2000-01 playoffs are double the player sum in 11 of 142 team-games (3 more in 2003-04). Team turnovers for 1985-95 regular seasons leave out team-charged turnovers.

    Would distort: Playoff possessions in 2000-01, and offensive Win Shares for 1985-95 (3-7% low).

  • CorrectedKaggle box-score dataset (copy of NBA records)

    Player-game rows for 1973-95: phantom 0-minute scorer rows (84 team-games in 1982-83), missing rows in 1984-95 (113 team-games, 778 points), short minutes in 1984-85, and bogus playoff rows.

    Would distort: Per-minute rates and games played in 1982-85, and point totals in 1984-95.

  • CorrectedNBA.com

    Live box-score files for 15 games froze as partial in-game snapshots (minutes far below 240), and live files are not updated after official stat corrections.

    Would distort: Minutes and games-missed reasons in those games.

  • Flagged where it is usedKaggle box-score dataset (copy of NBA records)

    Player box sums differ from the team box in about 40 team-games of 1996-2002 (steals, fouls, assists, blocks).

    Would distort: Team totals rebuilt from player rows, in under 0.1% of team-games per stat.

  • Flagged where it is usedKaggle box-score dataset (copy of NBA records)

    Points differ from 2 x FGM + 3PM + FTM in 44 player-game rows from 1955-56 to 1963-64.

    Would distort: Points before 1964, by a few.

Play-by-play2

Event-by-event game logs.

  • CorrectedKaggle box-score dataset (copy of NBA records)

    2019-20 play-by-play mixes two feed formats: substitutions do not balance in 221 games, only 12,053 of 16,200 steals carry a player, and 9 games are cut short.

    Would distort: 2019-20 lineup ratings and any steal-based measure that season.

  • CorrectedKaggle box-score dataset (copy of NBA records)

    Small play-by-play gaps: 3 games absent, 4,820 padded action labels in 2000-01, 3 impossible shot distances (up to 1,640 ft), and 2 games one point short.

    Would distort: Little beyond those games.

Tracking dashboards13

NBA.com tracking: hustle, speed and distance, defended shots, passes, matchups (who guarded whom) and Hawk-Eye shot logs.

  • CorrectedNBA.com tracking

    Catch-and-shoot and pull-up three-point makes and attempts are blank where the true value is zero.

    Would distort: Shooting on those shots for 316 and 257 player-seasons, if blanks are treated as missing.

  • CorrectedNBA.com tracking

    Corrupt tracking in three 2025-26 games: 39 regular-season player rows average above 6 mph, up to 22.6 mph.

    Would distort: Season speed and distance for every player in those games.

  • CorrectedNBA.com tracking

    The 2024-25 file of shots defended within 6 feet was served wrong on one day: 235 players had more attempts under 6 feet than under 10 feet (89,679 in all, against 71,396 two days later).

    Would distort: Rim-protection numbers for 2024-25.

  • CorrectedNBA.com tracking

    The 2015-16 regular-season hustle file covers 2 games but is served as a season. Box-outs and loose balls read 0 before 2017-18, when they were not tracked.

    Would distort: Hustle rates in 2015-16, and box-out trends that treat untracked zeros as real.

  • CorrectedNBA.com tracking

    Defended shot attempts sum to 108-112% of league attempts from 2017-18 to 2023-24, because several defenders share one shot.

    Would distort: Defended-shot totals compared across seasons.

  • CorrectedNBA.com tracking

    Tracking definitions changed in 2025-26: seconds per touch +34%, drives +49%, passes received per pass made from 1.000 to 0.883.

    Would distort: Year-over-year drive, touch and possession comparisons into 2025-26.

  • CorrectedNBA.com

    Matchup data (who guarded whom) doubles partial possessions in 69 games, mostly late in 2018-19.

    Would distort: Matchup possession counts, expected attempts and guarded-height averages.

  • CorrectedNBA.com tracking

    Season tracking rows carry the player's latest team. 77 rows name a team he never played for that season.

    Would distort: Team assignment and team-change flags.

  • CorrectedNBA.com (Hawk-Eye)

    The Hawk-Eye shot log returns 30 shots twice.

    Would distort: Shot counts for the players involved.

  • CorrectedNBA.com tracking

    Tracking files contradict themselves: play-type points per possession stored rounded in 18 files, rebound contest splits that do not sum to totals, and assists above potential assists on 102 rows.

    Would distort: Shares and rates above 100% unless clipped.

  • CorrectedNBA.com tracking

    The 2024-25 defended-threes dashboard serves a stale copy unless the request changes: 2-point plus 3-point attempts miss the total for 63% of players.

    Would distort: Three-point defense for 2024-25.

  • Flagged where it is usedNBA.com

    Definitions changed without notice: 2024-25 playoff tracking already uses the 2025-26 definitions, and play-by-play shot distance reads 0 ft for most corner threes in the legacy feed.

    Would distort: Any trend that pools playoff tracking or shot distance across the change.

  • CorrectedNBA.com

    The passes file includes players passing to themselves (34 rows per file), and bulk play-by-play has about 35 impossible shot coordinates.

    Would distort: Pass totals and shot charts, slightly.

Synergy play types3

Offensive and defensive play-type tables.

  • CorrectedNBA.com (Synergy play types)

    2023-24 offensive tables have no rows for some high-usage players, and regular-season files drop rows under 10 possessions without saying so.

    Would distort: Play-type shares and volumes, when a missing row is read as zero.

  • CorrectedNBA.com (Synergy play types)

    Defensive play types changed meaning in 2023-24: defensive possessions exceed offensive ones by 6-23%, and the ball-handler share jumped from 0.28 to 0.41.

    Would distort: Defensive play-type trends across 2023-24.

  • CorrectedNBA.com (Synergy play types)

    2025-26 play-type files count about 4% fewer games than the box score.

    Would distort: Play-type volume per game or per 36, 4-7% low.

Draft and combine6

Draft history, combine measurements and drills, public draft datasets.

  • CorrectedKaggle box-score dataset (copy of NBA records)

    The player file codes undrafted players as pick -1 or -22, and -22 also means "drafted, pick unknown" (1,323 players).

    Would distort: Draft-slot studies: undrafted players read as top picks. One era table counted 933 player-seasons as No. 1 picks.

  • CorrectedNBA.com

    Combine max vertical changed protocol around 2013: max minus standing went from about 4-5 in to 5.5-6.75 in. 8 rows show max below standing.

    Would distort: Leap percentiles pooled across eras.

  • CorrectedNBA.com

    Combine ids and measurements: 23 NBA players carry a non-NBA or wrong id, numeric and feet-inch fields disagree (one wingspan by 4 in), and 61 of 76 barefoot heights in 2007 are rounded down.

    Would distort: Links from the combine to NBA careers, and 2007 height and length measures.

  • CorrectedPublic pre-draft dataset

    2010 ages are class-year values (off by up to 1.34 years), the international flag is empty for 2010-15, and unranked prospects are coded 0.

    Would distort: Draft-age effects and international splits in draft studies.

  • Flagged where it is usedNBA.com

    From 2021, combine shooting drills are mislabelled: one spot holds the whole 25-shot spot-up drill, and one off-dribble field holds a 30-shot total.

    Would distort: Drill shooting by spot and range.

  • Flagged where it is usedNBA.com

    Draft history disagrees with the player file on 2 rows: a draft year, and a 10th-round pick given as round 0, pick 0.

    Would distort: Two 1950s draft records.

Player bios2

Birth dates, heights and player ids.

  • CorrectedKaggle box-score dataset (copy of NBA records)

    The player file holds 97 birth dates of 1900-01-01 and lists one player as 15 at his debut.

    Would distort: Ages, aging curves and age at draft.

  • CorrectedKaggle box-score dataset (copy of NBA records)

    Six players carry a second player id that NBA.com does not use, which splits their careers (one has 466 games under the old id).

    Would distort: Career totals and aging curves for those players.

College and international4

College (Barttorvik) and international league totals.

  • CorrectedBarttorvik

    Birth dates of the form YYYY-10-15 are class-based placeholders: 28.7% of a draft comparison pool, median error 151 days, max 2.3 years.

    Would distort: Age at draft for college prospects.

  • CorrectedBarttorvik

    One player id covers unrelated players (12 ids). NBA.com's awards feed also gives one player another same-name player's award.

    Would distort: College careers and award histories joined by player.

  • CorrectedBasketball-Reference

    French league and Spanish ACB 2025-26 totals do not add up: points differ from the sum of 2-point, 3-point and free-throw points on 331 rows.

    Would distort: Shooting splits and per-40 rates for players moving between those leagues and the NBA.

    Still to do: 130 of 268 French league players still lack a usable 2-point split.

  • CorrectedBarttorvik

    Per-game stats use a different game count from games played in 2008, 2015 and 2017-26. 15-20% of rows are off by more than 5%.

    Would distort: Season totals rebuilt from per-game numbers, and college-to-NBA translations.

Public player-rating sets.

  • CorrectedPublic metric panel

    One rating column is about a month older than the panel's posting date, and the file carries no dates.

    Would distort: Comparisons of public ratings on a fixed date.
