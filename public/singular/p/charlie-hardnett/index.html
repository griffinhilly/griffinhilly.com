How rough these ratings are

  • 1951-52 to 1972-73. Its box-score rating matches the full rating with R² 0.58 on held-out recent seasons (typical error 1.4). Defence is mostly invisible in these box scores, so great defenders, big men above all, are understated.

For his 3 seasons from 1962-63 to 1964-65 they are estimated from his box-score profile, and the box rating and SiPM* of those seasons use the estimates.
