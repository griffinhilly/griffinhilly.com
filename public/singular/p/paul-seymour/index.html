How rough these ratings are

  • 1951-52 to 1972-73. Box score without turnovers; steals, blocks and the offensive/defensive rebound split were not recorded and are estimated for each player (from his box-score profile and, for players who played on past 1973-74, his later recorded seasons), with 80% ranges; plus team results with and without the player; minutes often estimated. Its box-score rating matches the full rating with R² 0.58 on held-out recent seasons (typical error 1.4). Defence is mostly invisible in these box scores, so great defenders, big men above all, are understated.
  • Seasons before 1951-52 are not rated: the box scores before then record little more than points.
  • Steals, blocks and the split of rebounds into offensive and defensive were not recorded before 1973-74. For his 9 seasons from 1951-52 to 1959-60 they are estimated from his box-score profile, and the box rating and SiPM* of those seasons use the estimates. In 1953-54, for example, about 0.1 blocks (80% range 0.0 to 0.3) and 1.5 steals (1.0 to 2.0) per game. 80% ranges from the estimate alone: best season +1.1 to +2.6, best five +0.3 to +1.6, career +0.3 to +1.6; box score only: best season +1.1 to +2.8, best five +0.1 to +1.6.
