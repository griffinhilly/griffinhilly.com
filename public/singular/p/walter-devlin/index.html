How rough these ratings are

  • 1951-52 to 1972-73. Box score without turnovers; steals, blocks and the offensive/defensive rebound split were not recorded and are estimated for each player (from his box-score profile and, for players who played on past 1973-74, his later recorded seasons), with 80% ranges; plus team results with and without the player; minutes often estimated. Its box-score rating matches the full rating with R² 0.58 on held-out recent seasons (typical error 1.4). Defence is mostly invisible in these box scores, so great defenders, big men above all, are understated.
  • Steals, blocks and the split of rebounds into offensive and defensive were not recorded before 1973-74. For his 3 seasons from 1955-56 to 1957-58 they are estimated from his box-score profile, and the box rating and SiPM* of those seasons use the estimates. In 1955-56, for example, about 0.2 blocks (80% range 0.1 to 0.5) and 0.8 steals (0.5 to 1.1) per game. 80% ranges from the estimate alone: best season −1.4 to +0.2, career −1.9 to −0.3; box score only: best season −1.4 to +0.2.
