How rough these ratings are

  • 1951-52 to 1972-73. Box score without turnovers; steals, blocks and the offensive/defensive rebound split were not recorded and are estimated for each player (from his box-score profile and, for players who played on past 1973-74, his later recorded seasons), with 80% ranges; plus team results with and without the player; minutes often estimated. Its box-score rating matches the full rating with R² 0.58 on held-out recent seasons (typical error 1.4). Defence is mostly invisible in these box scores, so great defenders, big men above all, are understated.
  • 1973-74 to 1982-83. Box score with some stats filled from season totals, plus team results with and without the player. Its box-score rating matches the full rating with R² 0.70 on held-out recent seasons (typical error 1.2).
  • Steals, blocks and the split of rebounds into offensive and defensive were not recorded before 1973-74. For his 13 seasons from 1960-61 to 1972-73 they are estimated from his box-score profile and his recorded seasons from 1973-74 on, and the box rating and SiPM* of those seasons use the estimates. In 1963-64, for example, about 0.3 blocks (80% range 0.1 to 0.5) and 2.1 steals (1.5 to 2.8) per game. 80% ranges from the estimate alone: best season +5.8 to +7.2, best five +5.5 to +6.4, career +4.2 to +5.0; box score only: best season +6.1 to +7.5, best five +5.8 to +6.7.
