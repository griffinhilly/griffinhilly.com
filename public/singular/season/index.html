Careers: prime-based career figure

The all-time table's Career figure (prime-based, its opening view) for the 1,608 players with 3+ seasons of 1,000+ minutes.

Career, prime-based

Career starts from the prime: his minutes-weighted rating at ages 24-31. Three small adjustments sit on top, each a share of the prime. Load: minutes per season across the prime, measured against the star minutes of his era (from 10% off to 10% on). Early years (before 24) and late years (32 on): how he did against players with a similar prime and height at those ages. Ages before he was allowed into the league do not count. Seasons not yet played fill the prime at a cautious forecast (16th percentile); they can lower the early and late adjustments, never raise them. Playoffs count for a quarter of the prime: his playoff series at ages 24-31, each measured against the average player's playoff drop in that round, shrunk toward his regular-season prime by how many playoff minutes he has. Deep runs can lift him more than early ones; a poor late-round series never pulls him down harder than a poor first-round series would. † = an active player whose figure includes forecast seasons (hover for how many); player pages show the breakdown.
