About the 1971-72 values

What this season's box score can measure: box score without turnovers; steals, blocks and the offensive/defensive rebound split were not recorded and are estimated for each player (from his box-score profile and, for players who played on past 1973-74, his later recorded seasons); plus team results with and without the player; minutes often estimated.

Values are the all-time table's default view, Imputed lineup: his box-score rating plus an estimated lineup part. There is no lineup data before 1996-97, so that part is estimated from later players with similar box-score profiles and similar records of how their teams did with and without them; the estimate is cut back on purpose, so old stars are more likely understated than overstated.

Ratings are points per 100 possessions relative to that season's league: 0 is the season's average player, weighted by minutes played.
