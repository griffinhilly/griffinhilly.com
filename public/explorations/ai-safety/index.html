AI safety compendium

The fate of humanity may hinge on whether we're able to safely align superintelligent AI. Over a hundred unique ideas have been proposed for how to achieve this. The prospect of contributing to such a consequential project is alluring to many, but I myself spent too much time rederiving ideas that had already been offered by others before me. Therefore, I set out to map every idea in AI safety, so that I could start from the frontier and build upon it. This is that map.

Thus far, my attempted mapping has covered 218 sources, producing 129 unique ideas across 11 category groups.
