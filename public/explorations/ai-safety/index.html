AI safety compendium

Is humanity at the threshold of utopia, or destined for extinction? The answer may depend on the efforts of the people working on AI safety today. Over the last few decades, the field has produced wide-ranging views on how to make this effort go well. Rather than re-deriving the frontier of AI safety from first principles, I sought to map the territory. This is that map.
